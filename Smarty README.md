Weather Smarty streamlines weather warnings , alerts individuals of tornadic thunderstorms (supercells) , derechos, hurricanes in real time using AWS infrastructure.
This app makes use of AWS IOTcore, Sagemaker, Kinesis Data streams and will use SNS to allow for real time alerts.

The app will be built using OOP principles and there will be two versions of the app; Weather Smarty and Smarty pro 

Smarty pro uses AI and data analytics to provide weather suggestions for tourist and outdoor activities , it will use a proprietary engine called tellmetheBestweather, this ML model studies climates of various regions , counties municipalities globally and climates informing tourists when the weather will be good for hiking , surfing , going on the beach etc.
